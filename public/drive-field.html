How Filecoin Drive works

  1. Connect your wallet. Your browser is your workspace. Authorize a session and fund storage from Drive.
  2. Add your files. Each file gets a content identifier (CID), an address derived from its contents.
  3. Store on Filecoin. Independent storage providers hold the data, with storage proofs recorded on the network.
  4. Keep control. Browse, download and manage your files. Your content addresses work beyond this app.
